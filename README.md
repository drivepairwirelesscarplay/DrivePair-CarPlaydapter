The Voice We're Writing In
Friendly, confident, solution-focused. This is a knowledgeable friend who has already done the research and is sharing the verdict — not a brand rep reading from a spec sheet, and not a tech reviewer burying the lede in benchmark tables.
The reader knows their frustration is real. Acknowledge it without dwelling. Move quickly to the solution and let the value proposition carry the piece. Conversational but credible — every claim should feel earned, not asserted.
Actively Avoid
•	Technical jargon used without plain-language translation (e.g., "5GHz dual-band" needs a one-clause explanation of what that means for the driver)
•	Hype-y superlatives without data backup ("the best wireless CarPlay adapter ever made" — do not publish without citation)
•	Dismissive competitor framing — the comparison section should let the data make the case; editorializing feels insecure and alienates readers with prior investments in competing products
•	Preachy safety lectures — mention distraction reduction once, compellingly, then move on
